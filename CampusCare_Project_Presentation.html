Problem Analysis

Why Traditional Campus Maintenance Fails

Operational breakdowns in modern large-scale educational institutions

1. Lost Complaints & Physical Paper Logs

Students and faculty currently log broken fans, AC issues, or projector faults in paper registers at building receptions. Entries get misplaced, skipped, or forgotten without any timestamp or proof.

2. Zero Status Visibility & SLA Accountability

Complainants have no idea whether a technician was notified or when repairs will occur. Technicians face no enforced service-level agreements (SLAs), causing multi-day delays during exam or lecture hours.

3. Campus Spatial Ambiguity (Large Footprint)

GLBITM covers multiple massive blocks (AB-1, AB-2, AB-3, AB-4, Library, Hostels). Technicians waste 30 to 45 minutes simply searching for the exact room, wing, or faulty electrical panel.

4. Statutory Redressal Misrouting

Critical issues (safety, anti-ragging, mental health emergencies) cannot be treated like plumbing issues. Without automated guardrails, confidential matters risk delayed statutory action.

Speaker Script: "In a campus of over 10,000 students, paper complaint registers cause massive friction. Problems like broken projectors or lab power outages stall academics. There is no accountability, technicians don't know the exact room, and critical safety concerns risk being misrouted."

Role-Based Authentication (RBAC)

Protected by HTTP-only encrypted JSON Web Tokens (JWT). Distinct views and authority levels are enforced across Students, Maintenance Technicians, and College Administrators.

Real-Time Escalation Engine

If an urgent electrical failure or server room glitch exceeds its 2-hour resolution window, the system flags the ticket with red alert indicators and notifies the Maintenance Head.

Speaker Script: "The architectural flow is clean and robust. Our backend handles authentication via JWT, runs automated safety checks, and manages technician task boards. The automatic SLA clock guarantees that urgent tickets get immediate priority."

Future Roadmap

Scalability & Future Scope

Next evolution steps for smart campus infrastructure

🏷️ Physical QR-Code Desk Tags

Affixing QR code stickers to every classroom desk, projector stand, and water point. Scanning the code automatically loads the wizard with exact block, floor, and room number pre-selected.

🤖 AI Visual Damage Severity Assessment

Computer vision model to automatically grade photo evidence (e.g. water pipe burst vs slight drip) to dynamically adjust emergency urgency and pre-order replacement parts.

📡 IoT Smart Sensor Telemetry

Integrating hardware ultrasonic sensors on hostel water tanks and power grid sensors in electrical substations to autonomously dispatch tickets before outages affect campus life.

📱 Native Mobile App & Web Push

Instant push alerts dispatched to maintenance technicians' mobile phones, enabling one-tap job acceptance, camera photo capture, and on-site completion verification.

Speaker Script: "Looking ahead, CampusCare has massive growth potential. We envision QR codes on every classroom desk for 5-second reporting, AI photo analysis to classify damage severity, and IoT sensors on water tanks and power lines to detect issues proactively."
